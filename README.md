A quiet, earned moment of pride: a small person doing something hard, a
long effort finally landing. No trophy, no speech — a borrowed guitar with
two strings missing, eleven months of practice, and a song played all the
way through for the person it was learned for.

The entry, one line, 174 characters:

> Eleven months on a borrowed guitar with two strings missing, and tonight my little sister played her grandmother's favourite song all the way through, without missing a note.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program checks the line (one line, 1–240 characters), writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`, and prints it.

## Why this line

- **Genuine earned pride:** the pride is paid for up front — eleven months
  on a broken instrument — so the payoff feels deserved, not declared.
- **A concrete scene with a real person:** a little sister, her
  grandmother's favourite song, one night, one room.
- **Lands in one read:** the whole arc (effort, then the night it lands)
  fits in a single sentence with no detour.
- **Fresh:** the proof of the effort is a fact — not one missed note —
  rather than applause or a medal.
